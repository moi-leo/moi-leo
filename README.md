# Hello! I'm Su Bo'an😎

Senior English Teacher & Deputy Director, International Curriculum Center

Hangzhou Foreign Languages School

I am a senior English teacher at Hangzhou Foreign Languages School, where I have taught for over fifteen years. I hold a B.A. in English Language and Literature from Beijing Foreign Studies University and an M.A. in Applied Linguistics from the University of Edinburgh, with a research focus on pragmatic development in second language acquisition.

At HFLS, I teach advanced English reading, academic writing, and AP English Language and Composition. I led the development of our school-based Critical Reading and Writing curriculum, which integrates close textual analysis with structured argumentation training. My students have consistently earned top prizes in national English proficiency and public speaking competitions, and many have gone on to study at universities including Oxford, Cambridge, and Columbia.

Beyond the classroom, I have published seven papers in peer-reviewed journals on secondary EFL pedagogy and completed a provincial-level research project on cultivating critical thinking in high school English instruction (2019–2022). I also serve as faculty advisor for our Model United Nations program and contribute to teacher professional development initiatives within the school.

I believe the purpose of language education is not language itself, but the clearer thinking and more honest expression it makes possible.
